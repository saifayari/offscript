WHAT ARE TOKENS?

Models don't read whole sentences at once — they split text into small pieces called tokens.

Example Sentence
"Artificial Intelligence is amazing!"
Artificial
Token 1
·Intelligence
Token 2
·is
Token 3
·amazing
Token 4
!
Token 5
What can a token be? A common word, part of a word (subword), space (·), or punctuation mark.
Important: Actual tokenization depends on the specific model and its vocabulary.

ANATOMY OF A GOOD PROMPT

1 🎭
ROLE
Who should AI act as?
"Act as a senior marketing strategist"
2 🌍
CONTEXT
What does it need to know?
"For an artisan café in Tunis"
3 ⚡
TASK
What should it do?
"Create a 2-week growth plan"
4 🚧
CONSTRAINTS
What limitations exist?
"Under 150 TND, organic content only"
5 📋
OUTPUT FORMAT
How should the result look?
"Bullet list + 3 ready captions"
Complete Formula in Action
Act as a senior marketing strategist. I run a small artisan café in Tunis targeting students. Create a 2-week customer growth plan. Keep the budget under 150 TND using organic social media. Present it as a bullet list plus 3 Instagram captions.

WEAK PROMPT VS GOOD PROMPT

✕ WEAK PROMPT No context · No constraints
"Give me a business idea."
Typical AI Output
"1. Start an online store.
2. Open a restaurant.
3. Offer consulting services…"
Too Generic Wrong Budget Not Actionable
✓ GOOD PROMPT Role · Context · Budget · Audience · Format
"Act as a startup advisor. I am a student in Tunisia launching a side project for university students with a budget under 300 TND. Suggest 3 realistic business ideas in a comparison table with first launch steps."
